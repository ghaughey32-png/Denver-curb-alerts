# Boston sign check, round 2: 10 more untested blocks (2026-10-26)

A second draw of ten blocks from the rows the station check could not test (seed 20261027; the ten already
checked in [BOSTON-SIGN-CHECK.md](BOSTON-SIGN-CHECK.md) are excluded, and block 1 here, Madeline St, was
looked at in the built-in browser and not found: see its note).

**How to use the links.** Each block has several Street View links spaced about 35 m apart along the curb we
say has the rule, each opened already facing that curb (heading and field of view are in the link). Open one,
look for a street-cleaning sign on a pole at the curb, and zoom in. If you do not see a pole, try the next
link, or turn around: the pole may be a few metres past the edge of the frame. A match means the sign agrees
with "We say"; the twin row is shown because a sign on the opposite curb is the twin's. A missing sign or an
unreadable image is "no sign / unreadable", not a miss. Record the house number on the pole's side when you can:
the camera label is only the nearest address and has been the wrong parity three times out of ten.
