How it works

From parked car to protected curb in under a minute.

  1. 1
    Find your block. Use your location, search an address, or pan the map.
  2. 2
    Tap the curb where you park. Each side of the street is separate, because Denver sweeps them on different days. The next sweep date comes up straight away. Just got out of the car? Tap Park here and the reminders follow that spot.
  3. 3
    Turn on reminders. The evening before and the morning of, until you tell us the car is moved.

Terms of use

Terms for Denver Curb Alerts

Last updated September 11, 2026

Denver Curb Alerts is a reminder tool built to help drivers remember posted Denver street sweeping restrictions. It is operated by Curb Alerts LLC ("we" and "us" in these terms). By using the app, you agree to use it as an extra reminder and not as a replacement for checking posted signs, city rules, or your own parking situation.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for parking legally. Street signs, temporary city notices, emergency restrictions, construction, weather, and official Denver rules always control over anything shown in this app.

What it costs

Nothing. The map, address search, curb colors, saved curb sets, accounts, and street sweeping reminders are all free, and there is no way to pay us. We do not ask for a card, we do not store one, and no part of the app is behind a paywall.

If that ever changes, reminders will not be what changes. An alert that stops you getting a ticket is never going to be withheld over money.

Changes to the app

We may change, add, pause, or stop features as the app improves.

Curb coverage is best effort and always will be. Denver's published sweeping data is incomplete in places, the city changes schedules, and nothing here guarantees that a given block is correct or that a reminder will arrive. See the Disclaimer for what that means in practice.

Acceptable use

Please do not misuse the app, interfere with the service, scrape it aggressively, submit false reports, or use it in a way that could harm other users or the city data services it relies on.

Contact

For questions about your account or your data, email support@curbalerts.co. For map corrections and bug reports, the in-app Report an issue form reaches us fastest.

Privacy

Privacy for Denver Curb Alerts

Last updated September 15, 2026

Denver Curb Alerts is operated by Curb Alerts LLC, which is the "we" on this page.

We collect only what the app needs to help you create and receive curb reminders. Your location is used to center the map and to remember where you parked, and that parking spot stays on your device. We do not sell your data.

Information the app may use

  • The curb sides you turn reminders on for, and reminder times, so the app can build your alert schedule.
  • Your browser push subscription, so reminders can be delivered to the device you connected.
  • Your email address and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password, only if you choose to create an account, so your saved curb sets can follow you to another device. We never store your actual password.
  • Your approximate or precise location only when you tap Use my location, so the map can move to you.
  • Where you parked, only when you tap Park here or I parked here. The pin is kept on your device and cleared when you move your car or clear it yourself. If reminders reach you by browser push, the schedule we hold names the street and side of the curb, never the pin's coordinates.
  • Issue reports you choose to send, including your note and helpful app context.

How information is used

We use this information to show the map, save your alert setup, send test notifications, schedule reminders, confirm your email address, let you reset a forgotten password, troubleshoot bugs, and improve curb coverage. We do not use it for advertising.

Services involved

The app is hosted on Render, which also hosts the database holding accounts and reminder schedules. It uses browser push services operated by your browser vendor, map tiles and street data from OpenStreetMap, and street sweeping data published by the City and County of Denver. Those services may process basic technical information needed to load maps, routes, and notifications.

One more is involved only if you create an account: Resend delivers the confirmation and password reset emails, which means your email address passes through it. There is no payment processor, because there are no payments.

Keeping and deleting your data

Deleting your account from the Account page removes the account, its saved curb sets, every signed-in session, and any outstanding email links, immediately and for good. Reminders you set up on a device keep running unless you turn them off, because they belong to the device rather than to the account.

If you want a copy of what we hold, or want it removed without using the in-app delete, email us at the address below.

Your choices

You can decline location access, decline notifications, turn off curb reminders, delete saved sets, delete your account, or remove the app from your device.

Contact

For privacy questions or a data request, email support@curbalerts.co, or use Report an issue.

One-time setup

Add Curb Alerts to your Home Screen

iPhone only sends reminders from apps on your Home Screen. This takes about fifteen seconds.

  1. 1
    Tap the Share button in Safari. It's the square with the arrow, in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
  2. 2
    Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen". It sits below the row of share icons, with a plus in a square.
  3. 3
    Open Curb Alerts from the new icon. Come back here from that icon and the reminder button will work.
