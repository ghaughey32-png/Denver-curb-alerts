How it works

From parked car to protected curb in under a minute.

  1. 1
    Find your block. Use your location, search an address, or pan the map.
  2. 2
    Tap the curb where you park. Each side of the street is separate, because Denver sweeps them on different days. The next sweep date comes up straight away.
  3. 3
    Turn on reminders. One the evening before, one on the morning of.

Privacy

Privacy for Denver Curb Alerts

Last updated August 4, 2026

We collect only what this beta needs to help you create and receive curb reminders. Your location is only used to center the map. We do not sell your data.

Information the app may use

  • Your selected curb sides and reminder times, so the app can build your alert schedule.
  • Your browser push subscription, so reminders can be delivered to the device you connected.
  • Your approximate or precise location only when you tap Use my location, so the map can move to you.
  • Issue reports you choose to send, including your note and helpful app context.

How information is used

We use this information to show the map, save your alert setup, send test notifications, schedule reminders, troubleshoot bugs, and improve curb coverage.

Services involved

The app is hosted on Render and uses browser push services, map tiles, and Denver street sweeping data. Those services may process basic technical information needed to load maps, routes, and notifications.

Your choices

You can decline location access, decline notifications, clear selections, delete saved sets, or remove the app from your device. For now, use Report an issue if you want us to review or remove stored beta data tied to your device.

One-time setup

Add Curb Alerts to your Home Screen

iPhone only sends reminders from apps on your Home Screen. This takes about fifteen seconds.

  1. 1
    Tap the Share button in Safari. It's the square with the arrow, in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
  2. 2
    Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen". It sits below the row of share icons, with a plus in a square.
  3. 3
    Open Curb Alerts from the new icon. Come back here from that icon and the reminder button will work.
