How it works

From parked car to protected curb in under a minute.

  1. 1
    Tap the curb where you park. Search your address, use your location, or pan the Sloan's Lake map.
  2. 2
    Choose the exact side. Colored lines show the curb side you are selecting, not just the street name.
  3. 3
    Save your reminders. Connect push and queue day-before plus day-of reminders for the next sweep.
